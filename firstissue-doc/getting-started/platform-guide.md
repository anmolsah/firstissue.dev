FirstIssue.dev is your gateway to discovering amazing open source opportunities. This guide covers all the platform features to help you make the most of your experience.

## Explore Page - Finding Issues

The Explore page is where you'll discover beginner-friendly issues from across GitHub:

- Use filters to narrow down issues by language, labels, and project size
- Sort by recency, popularity, or difficulty
- Preview issue details without leaving the page
- Select 'View on GitHub' to access the external repository

## Bookmarks - Save for Later

Found interesting issues but not ready to work on them? Use bookmarks:

1. Click the bookmark icon () on any issue
2. Visit your Bookmarks page to see all saved issues
3. Update status as you progress: Saved → Applied → Working On → Done
4. Filter your bookmarks by status or language

## Status Tracking

Track your contribution journey with our status system:

- Saved - Issues saved for future consideration
- Applied - Issues where interest has been declared or comments made
- Working On - Issues actively in development
- Done - Successfully merged or resolved contributions

## Profile & Statistics

Your profile page shows your open source journey:

- Contribution statistics and development progress
- Achievement badges and user milestones
- Languages and technologies utilized in contributions
- Personal contribution goals and developmental targets

> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> Regularly update your issue statuses to keep track of your progress and help us recommend better issues for you!

## Proof of Work (PoW) Attestations

To combat resume padding and prove actual contribution impact, FirstIssue.dev introduces cryptographic Proof of Work (PoW) Attestations. You can input any merged GitHub pull request URL to verify it automatically.

-  Paste your merged PR URL in the Proof of Work tab
- Verification is executed instantly via a secure Supabase Edge Function
- A cryptographically signed attestation is stored, verifying ownership of the contribution
- Earn an impact score based on the contribution size and complexity

## 3D MetalCards & Impact Showcase

Every verified Proof of Work is minted into a premium, interactive 3D MetalCard. These cards visually showcase your accomplishments:

- Interactive 3D card layouts responding to hover movements
- Color-coded by the primary programming language of the pull request
- Displays repository details, PR number, title, hash, and impact score
- Downloadable as a high-quality PNG for external portfolios

## Public Developer Profiles & Freemium Limits

Your contributions and MetalCards are aggregated on your Public Developer Profile page. Share your unique link (e.g., /u/your-username) with recruiters, team leads, or on your social profiles to show off your verified portfolio.

> [!WARNING] Freemium Limits
> To ensure system resources are fairly distributed, free tier accounts are limited to minting 1 free Proof of Work credential. To unlock unlimited verified attestations and 3D MetalCards, you can upgrade to the Supporter plan at any time.
